# Documents to request, and red flags

Built from what came up on Tumble Wash. Ask for the first group on the first broker call; the rest follow once an LOI is signed.

## Documents to request

### First call (before any offer)
- [ ] **Lease**, all amendments, and the landlord's name. Ask whether the landlord will consent to an assignment and what guaranty they will want.
- [ ] **Card-system reports, 24–36 months, monthly**, exported directly from the system (on Tumble Wash: SD CCI), by machine if possible. Coin store: water bills plus supervised collections instead.
- [ ] **Wash-and-fold POS reports**, same period (on Tumble Wash: Cents): pounds, orders, top accounts, pickup vs. drop-off.
- [ ] **Utility bills, 12–24 months**: water and sewer, gas, electric. Get the meter arrangement in writing if the building shares a meter.
- [ ] **Tax returns, 3 years** (Schedule C or business return), and the books the proforma came from.
- [ ] **Equipment list**: make, model, serial number, size and age of every machine, plus repair and service records for 24 months.
- [ ] **Payroll records** for every employee (hours, rates, payroll tax filings).

### After the LOI (diligence, 14–21 days)
- [ ] Data-plate photos of every machine, and a **distributor technician's inspection** (bearings, seals and spin; dryer burners and drums).
- [ ] **Water heater and boiler** age and service history. HVAC age.
- [ ] Bank deposits reconciled against the card-system reports and the tax returns.
- [ ] Vending, ATM and arcade machines: which are owned (they transfer) and which are revenue-share (they don't). Who owns and operates any Bitcoin ATM.
- [ ] Licenses and permits (mercantile license, vending and sales tax registration, health and fire).
- [ ] Commercial and wash-and-fold accounts: list, volumes, and whether they transfer.
- [ ] Insurance loss history; any claims, police calls or pest (bedbug) problems.
- [ ] Seller's purchase price and what they changed since buying (card system, POS, pickup are often their additions).

### Lease points to check (attorney reads the whole thing)
- [ ] Firm remaining term vs. base term plus options, and the option rent.
- [ ] Assignment clause: can consent be unreasonably withheld, fees, does the seller stay liable.
- [ ] **Personal guaranty**: who must sign. Cap it at 12–24 months of rent or use a "good guy" guaranty.
- [ ] **Demolition, relocation or redevelopment clause.** In a fast-redeveloping city this can wipe out the investment. If one exists, require a long notice period and a buyout.
- [ ] Use clause (wash-and-fold, delivery and vending allowed) and an exclusive (no other laundromat in the property).
- [ ] What "NNN + CAM included" really covers: sewer, water, property tax pass-throughs.
- [ ] Who repairs what: plumbing, drains, HVAC, roof, water heater.
- [ ] Consent for future equipment alterations, gas lines and venting.
- [ ] Security deposit and whether it transfers.

## Red flags

| Red flag | Why it matters | What to do | Tumble Wash |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Payroll tax and workers' comp shown as N/A** | Both are legally required. In NJ they add ~13–16% to wages | Add them back in the recast | Both N/A; ~$11–12k/yr missing |
| **No electric line, or "included", or a shared meter** | Often $10–18k/yr for a ~2,000 sf store | Get 12 months of bills and the landlord's split in writing; model it as `ElecExtra` | Landlord paid $340/mo on one building meter, the store the rest; P&L showed $0 |
| **Sewer "included in rent"** | Sewer is usually billed on metered water, so it is often as large as the water bill | Confirm in the lease | Claimed included; unverified |
| **P&L total doesn't match its own lines** | Shows how carefully, or loosely, the numbers were built | Re-add every line | $15,300/yr short |
| **Seller's hours not paid in the P&L, or paid at minimum wage with no burden** | "Absentee" profit counts labor someone must do | Pay every attended hour at a wage that keeps staff | 91 paid hrs at $16.50, just over the 2027 minimum of $16.48 |
| **Repairs under ~3% of revenue on an old fleet** | Owner is doing repairs unpaid, or deferring them | Use 5–7% of revenue for a fleet over 10 years old | $5.4k/yr (1.4%) on a 15-year fleet |
| **Insurance under ~1.5% of revenue** | Underinsured, or not counted | Get a quote | $2,880/yr vs. $5–9k realistic |
| **Washers 12–15+ years old** | That is the industry-average life (CLA survey); Speed Queens can reach 18–25 years but that's the best case | Budget replacement within 1–4 years; push on price; get the inspection | ~15 years: ~$373k of washers in years 1–4 |
| **Rent above ~25% of revenue** | Rule-of-thumb ceiling; above ~33% is weak | Compare to comps (median ~22%) | 8.9%, the best in the comp set |
| **Lease under ~10 years left, or "years left" includes options** | Value and resale depend on the lease; buyers avoid under 10 years | Confirm firm term and option rent; get landlord consent before closing | 17 years, unconfirmed whether firm |
| **No card or POS data, or the seller won't share it** | Revenue can't be verified | Water analysis plus supervised collections, or walk | Card store with CCI + Cents: a strength |
| **Broker SDE well above ~35% of revenue** | The comp median is ~35%; much higher usually means missing expenses | Recast before quoting any multiple | 48% |
| **Cash-only sale (no SBA, bank or seller financing)** | Lenders underwrite to tax returns, so cash-only can mean the returns show less | Reconcile reports, deposits and returns | Cash only |
| **Unverifiable cash lines (games, vending, ATM, dry cleaning)** | Easy to inflate | Haircut ~15% (`AncRealize`) | ~$40k/yr of such lines |
| **Machine or count mismatches between listing and P&L** | Sloppy, or the store changed | Count in person | 34 vs. 36 dryers |
| **Strong competitor within a few blocks, or a chain renovating nearby** | Can take 10–20% of self-serve | Stress-test self-serve at −15% | Bubbles 2 one block away with 220 reviews |
| **Goodwill tied to the seller personally** | Customers and staff may leave with them | Written training period, staff retention, meet regulars | Reviews credited "a kind loving family" |
| **Seller selling just before a big equipment bill** | They're handing you the capex | Price the capex in | Likely |
| **"It does more than the tax returns show"** | Admits underreporting to the IRS; assume the same toward you | Underwrite to verified records only | n/a |
| **Bitcoin ATM in the store** | Widely used in scams; growing regulation | Find out who owns it and what it earns; consider removing it | Present |
