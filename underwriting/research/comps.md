**Red flags and diligence points (my analysis):**
- **Weekly revenue doesn't add up to the stated gross.** ($4,150 + $2,300) × 52 = **$335,400**, not $372,000. The ~$37k gap needs an explanation (vending? seasonality? pre-tax?).
- **The SDE margin is 47.6%.** That is far above the typical 20–35% net margin and the BizBuySell median of 35% (SDE $76.6k on $220k revenue). The seller works 50 hrs/week, and replacing those hours at about $17–18/hr in NJ (2026 minimum wage is about $15.92+) plus payroll tax costs about $50k/yr. The broker says payroll is "adjusted", so check whether it is actually deducted.
- **The same broker has an identical template in the Bronx.** Ad #2564395 also asks $450k on $177k SDE, with "family obligations" as the reason. Treat the round-number pricing skeptically.
- **Wash & fold at $1.25/lb is below the CLA 2023 survey mode** of $1.50–1.99/lb (drop-off). That is pricing upside.
- **15-year-old equipment** will push value lower under rule-of-thumb guidance (13–16 yrs gives 3.5–4x NOI, before considering the replacement capex need). The long lease (15+ yrs gives 4.5–5x) and very low rent (≤25% gives 4.5–5x) push value higher.

## 3. Valuation rules of thumb (how lease and equipment move price)

From [Laundromat Resource, "How Much is My Laundromat Worth?"](https://www.laundromatresource.com/?p=13318) (multiples of NOI, before debt and taxes):

| Factor | Higher multiple (4.5–5x) | Lower multiple (3.5–4x) |
|---|---|---|
| Base | | 3.5–5x NOI overall |
| Equipment age | 0–5 yr | ~13–16 yr; older can fall below 3.5x |
| Lease remaining | 15+ yr | ~10 yr; buyers should avoid leases under 10 yr |
| Rent (incl. NNN/CAM) as % of gross | ≤25% | ≥33% |
| Market | +0.25–0.5x in a seller's market | −0.25–0.5x in a buyer's market |

Applied to the subject: equipment 15 yr (3.5–4x), lease 17 yr (4.5–5x), rent ~9% (4.5–5x). The blended rule of thumb is about **4–4.5x true NOI**, less a capex reserve for replacing equipment within about 3–7 years. The comps show older-equipment stores trading at 1.4–3.3x (Old Lyme, Paterson, Bellmore).

Other references: [Cents, How to value your laundromat](https://www.trycents.com/our-2-cents/how-to-value-your-laundromat-to-sell-it); [Iconic, laundromat valuation](https://iconic.co/blog/laundromat-valuation/).

## 5. Verified vs. inferred

- **Verified (read directly from a source page):** all BizBuySell national benchmarks; JC #2564518 details; Bronx #2564395; the Bergen 4,000 sf expense lines; HedgeStone 62794 and 63086; Vested 7091 and 7167; the Laundromat-Broker.com table; the DealStream NJ list; American Coin-Op vend prices and utility percentages; CLA 2023 W&F and rent figures.
- **From search-result summaries only (detail page not opened):** most Section B NYC rows, Bayonne, Ridgefield Park and Wallington.
- **Inferred or calculated by me:** all multiples; Union County gross ($8k/wk × 52); West Hartford gross; the JC revenue reconciliation ($335k); the JC rent-to-gross percentage.
- **Sold prices are asking prices.** None of these sources disclose the actual closing price. BizBuySell's national sale/ask ratio of 0.92 suggests discounting by about 8%.
