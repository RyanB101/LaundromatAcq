### 1.6 Worked example: fully loaded attendant cost

Assumes $17.00/hr and a full-time attendant (2,080 hrs, $35,360 wages).

| Component | % | $ |
|---|---|---|
| FICA | 7.65% | 2,705 |
| NJ UI (new employer) | 2.6825% | 949 |
| NJ WF/SWF | 0.1175% | 42 |
| NJ SDI | 0.50% | 177 |
| JC payroll tax (non-JC-resident employee) | 1.0% | 354 |
| FUTA | flat | 42 |
| Workers' comp (est. 2.0%) | 2.0% | 707 |
| Earned sick leave (40 hrs) | ~1.9% | 680 |
| **Total burden** | **~16.0%** | **~5,656** |
| **Loaded cost** | | **~$41,000/yr, about $19.70/hr** |

Coverage math: staffing every hour of a 7am–10pm, 7-day store takes 105 hrs/wk, or 5,460 hrs/yr. At $19.70/hr that costs about **$107,500/yr**. Each hour per day of coverage removed saves about $7,200/yr. At the 2027 minimum ($16.48 + 16%) the loaded floor is about **$19.10/hr**.

### 2.4 Utility benchmarks (% of gross revenue)
- **American Coin-Op 2023-24 survey:** utilities average **21.6%** of gross. Most common answer 20%. 60% of stores at 20% or less ([American Coin-Op](https://americancoinop.com/articles/2023-24-state-industry-survey-part-3)).
- **Cents:** about **15%** with new machines, about **20%** with older ones ([Cents](https://trycents.com/our-2-cents/laundromat-utility-costs)).
- **CLA/PlanetLaundry, May 2025:** utilities average **21%**. Efficient operators run under 13% ([PlanetLaundry May 2025](https://digitaleditions.walsworth.com/article/Cover+Story/4972007/845385/article.html)).
- **NJ adjustment:** NJ commercial electricity costs about 26% above the US average, and JCMUA water/sewer is high. **Underwrite at 20–25% of gross for older equipment and 15–18% after an equipment refresh.**
